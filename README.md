# Flow-Rate-Measurement-of-Self-Compacting-Concrete-Using-Chirped-Fiber-Bragg-Grating-Sensors

🚧 **Final Year B.Tech Project** | 📍 *NIT Warangal – Dept. of ECE*

---

## 📌 **Project Overview**

This project introduces a **high-sensitivity optical sensing system** to monitor the flow dynamics of **Self-Compacting Concrete (SCC)** in real time using **Chirped Fiber Bragg Grating (CFBG)** sensors. Designed for structural health monitoring (SHM) and smart construction, our system addresses limitations of traditional sensors such as EMI interference and poor resolution.

By combining **advanced signal processing** and **fiber-optic sensing**, the system enables early detection of flow irregularities like voids or segregation—critical for modern infrastructure durability.  

---

## 🚀 **Key Features**

- 🔬 **Sensor Type**: Chirped Fiber Bragg Grating (CFBG)  
- 📏 **Strain Sensitivity**: `2 pm/µε`  
- 💧 **Flow Rate Sensitivity**: `0.006454 nm/(m³/s)`  
- 🧠 **Denoising Algorithm**: HMNS-PP (Butterworth + Wavelet + Gaussian + MA Filter)  
- 🌡️ **Temperature Compensation**: Dual-FBG configuration  
- 🔗 **Multiplexing Support**: Distributed sensing over a single optical fiber  
- 🔒 **EMI Immunity**: Fully optical & non-intrusive system

---

## 🏗️ **Applications**

- 🧱 Real-time monitoring of SCC flow during pouring  
- 📉 Detection of defects: segregation, air voids, improper compaction  
- 🧪 Automated, in-situ quality control in construction  
- 🏙️ Long-term structural health monitoring for civil infrastructure
